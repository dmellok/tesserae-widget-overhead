# Attribution

- **Positions**: adsb.lol (https://adsb.lol), a community ADS-B aggregator, with adsb.fi (https://adsb.fi) asked when adsb.lol is slow or down; or your own receiver.
- **Routes**: adsbdb (https://www.adsbdb.com).
- **Photographs**: planespotters.net (https://www.planespotters.net/photo/api), each credited to its photographer on the page; the widget sends a User-Agent with the contact you give in the settings, as the API requires, and shows the photographs only within your own install.
- **Airline marks** (only when Airline logos is on): fetched at render time from Daisycon (https://www.daisycon.io) or Kiwi.com, never stored with the widget. The marks belong to the airlines.
- **Fonts**: IM Fell English and IM Fell English SC by Igino Marini (SIL Open Font License), Archivo Narrow by Omnibus-Type (SIL Open Font License). Licences in overhead_core/lib/fonts/.
- **Silhouettes** (overhead_core/static/sil/): drawn for this widget; MIT with the rest.
- **Visitors' reference pictures**: the craft a page may add are painted from openly licensed pictures on Wikimedia Commons (CC and public domain, each named with its author and licence in overhead_core/lib/paint.py, UFO_REFS) and NASA; the painting's credit on the page names them.
- **AI paintings**: made on your own fal.ai account from the photographs above; the painting's credit names the model and the photographer.
